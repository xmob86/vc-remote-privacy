V.C Remote — Privacy Policy

App: V.C Remote (package vc.smartremote) · Last updated: September 29, 2026

V.C Remote ("the App") is a remote-control app for Android TV boxes. This policy explains what data the App handles and how it is used. It applies to the App itself, not to third-party sites you may visit.

1. Data the App collects or uses

The App is designed to work entirely on your local network. We (the developer) do not collect, receive, or store personal information about you.

The App works with the following data, stored only on your device and never transmitted to us:

  • Device names and network addresses — the name and IP address of the TV boxes you add. These are used only to connect to your boxes over your own Wi-Fi network and to re-pair automatically on future launches.
  • Local preferences — settings such as your saved boxes, are kept in the App's private storage on your device only.

2. Advertising (AdMob)

The App displays mobile ads served by Google AdMob. Google may receive and use certain information (such as device model, Android version, and, where enabled, the Google Advertising ID) to select and serve ads, in accordance with Google's advertising policies.

  • We do not control or see the ads served; we do not receive any personal data from them.
  • You can opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (Settings → Ads) or at adssettings.google.com.
  • Google may use SDKs that collect technical information for fraud prevention and attribution, as described in Google's AdMob privacy documentation.

3. Data we do NOT collect

The App does not collect or require: accounts or email addresses, names, contacts, location, SMS, call history, or any content from other apps. It requests only the network permissions needed to reach your TV box on the same Wi-Fi network (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE).

4. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with anyone. The only third party that may receive technical data in connection with the App is Google (AdMob), as described above.

5. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

6. Your rights

All App data (saved boxes, preferences) lives only on your device. You can delete it at any time by uninstalling the App or clearing its data in Android settings. No request to us is needed to erase your data, because we hold none.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App changes. The "Last updated" date above reflects the current version. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy or the App: easyweathertask@gmail.com (or contact us via the app's Google Play listing).
